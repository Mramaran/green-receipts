How the number is calculated

Method using .

  • Energy is a min–max range from the open-source estimator, based on the model's output tokens and request latency. It is estimated, not metered.
  • Grid intensity is the hourly value for the region the caller declares, in gCO₂e per kWh.
  • Embodied emissions (making the hardware) come from the estimator and are added once per request.

How wide the error range is

The lower bound uses the lowest energy estimate and the intensity reduced by %. The upper bound uses the highest energy estimate and the intensity raised by %. Embodied emissions are added to both.

The grid-intensity uncertainty is an assumption, not a measured error. The range reflects the spread the estimator reports and that assumption, nothing more.

    Verifying a receipt yourself

    1. Render the receipt as canonical JSON: keys sorted, no whitespace, ASCII only, numbers as plain decimals with no exponent and no trailing zeros.
    2. Hash it with keccak256.
    3. Recover the signer from the signature over that hash (Ethereum signed-message format).
    4. Ask the contract for the record of that hash. It must exist, its recorded issuer must be the signer, and its recorded organisation and emissions range must match the receipt. Any edited field produces a different hash, so an edited receipt is not found.

    The server runs the same checks at POST /v1/verify. scripts/verify_standalone.py runs them without our server, directly against a node, using only the downloaded receipt file.
